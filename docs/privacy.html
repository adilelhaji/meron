Oreneta Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

Oreneta is a desktop application. It has no server of its own, no analytics, and no telemetry. Your mail, calendars, and credentials stay on your computer and travel only between your computer and the mail providers you sign in to.

What Oreneta stores, and where

  • Messages and calendar events are cached in a local database on your computer, so the app works fast and offline.
  • Credentials (passwords, OAuth tokens) are stored in your operating system's keychain, or in a local encrypted file where no keychain is available.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere except to the mail and calendar servers of the accounts you add.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Oreneta requests access to Gmail (to read and send your mail over IMAP/SMTP), your basic profile (to label the account with your name and address), and Google Calendar (to show, create, edit and delete events you ask it to).

Oreneta's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the mail and calendar features you see, on your own computer.
  • It is never transferred to third parties, never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train models.
  • No human other than you reads it.

Revoking access

Remove an account in Oreneta's settings and its local data is deleted. You can also revoke Oreneta's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
