Oreneta

Mail and calendar, at home with Exchange.

What it is

Oreneta is a fast desktop mail and calendar app with chat and kanban views, and native Microsoft Exchange (EWS) support — no IMAP bridge in between. Oreneta is Catalan for the swallow, the bird that always finds its way home.

What it does

  • Mail over IMAP, Gmail, Outlook, and native Exchange (EWS).
  • A full calendar: agenda, day, week and month views; recurring series expanded by the server, never guessed at.
  • An account's calendars arrive with the account; local calendars and read-only .ics subscriptions come along too.
  • Everything stays on your computer — there is no Oreneta server.
